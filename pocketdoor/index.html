Your phone, on your PC

Pocket Door links your Android phone to your Windows PC. Notifications, copied text, photos and files move between them over your own Wi-Fi. Nothing goes through anyone else's servers.

What it does

Notifications you can answer

Reply and use their buttons without picking up your phone.

Copy there, paste here

Copied text crosses over, both ways.

Photos and files

Your latest photos by the clock. Drag files to send them.

Get Pocket Door

For WindowsDownload for WindowsRun it. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway. It installs itself and keeps itself up to date.
For AndroidDownload for AndroidDownload it on your phone and open it, allowing your browser to install apps. If Android later says a setting is restricted, open Settings, Apps, Pocket Door, tap the three dots and Allow restricted settings.

Pocket Door has no accounts and no servers of its own: your phone and your PC talk only to each other. Privacy policy
